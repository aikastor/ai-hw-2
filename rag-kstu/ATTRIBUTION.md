# University document attribution

The reviewed PDFs, excerpts, tables and metadata identify Kyrgyz State Technical University named after I. Razzakov and their original official URLs in `data/source-manifest.json` and chunk citations. These third-party university materials are preserved for this educational RAG assignment. Application authors do not claim ownership of university documents or grant additional rights over them.

The tokenizer asset is the cl100k_base vocabulary used by tiktoken. Upstream information is at https://github.com/openai/tiktoken. Dependencies and downloaded models retain their upstream terms. Model weights and Ollama binaries are excluded from this repository.
